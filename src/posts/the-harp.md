I rented the harp on a whim. The website said &ldquo;beginner friendly&rdquo; and I, a beginner,
felt addressed. I believed the website. The website lied gently and beautifully, the way
websites do.

A harp, it turns out, is **forty-seven strings**. Forty-seven! I had assumed it was a
manageable number, like twelve. There is no manageable number when each string is also
slightly out of tune with the other forty-six, all of the time, on principle.

## Things I have learned

- The harp is louder than you think.
- The harp is louder than your neighbours think.
- &ldquo;Clair de Lune&rdquo; is not, as I had been led to believe, *for beginners*.
- My cat is, on balance, against this.

## In defence of the harp

I will say this: when I get something right &mdash; even four notes, even briefly &mdash;
it sounds like the inside of a small cathedral. Then I get the next note wrong and it
sounds like the cathedral has been mugged. But for those four notes? Worth it. Probably.
Ask me again in March.
