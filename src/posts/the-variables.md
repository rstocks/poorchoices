The first sign that **The Variables** was going to be a problem came when our drummer, Pat,
asked &mdash; reasonably &mdash; what the name meant. I said it was a maths joke. Pat said,
&ldquo;Right, but what's the *joke*.&rdquo;

I did not have an answer. I still do not have an answer.

## The flyer

I designed the flyer myself, which is its own line item in the broader regret. I used a
font I had downloaded that morning. The venue's owner, a man named Greg who has seen many
bands, looked at it for some time and then said: &ldquo;Mate.&rdquo;

> I assumed this was praise. It was not praise.

We played to thirteen people, four of whom were the other band. One of the remaining nine
left during our second song to take a phone call and did not return. I have thought about
that phone call almost every day since.

## What I would have named us instead

Anything. Genuinely anything. &ldquo;Two Beers Please.&rdquo; &ldquo;The Sound Check.&rdquo;
&ldquo;Greg.&rdquo; In hindsight, &ldquo;Greg&rdquo; would have at least gotten us a second
booking on the strength of the in-joke alone.

The band broke up six weeks later, citing &ldquo;creative differences,&rdquo; which is
what you say when the creative difference is that one person named the band
*The Variables*.
