There is a particular silence that follows hitting *Reply All* on an email you intended to
send to one person. The silence is not in the office. The office gets louder. The silence
is somewhere behind your eyes.

I will not reproduce the email here. I will say that it contained the phrase &ldquo;between
you and me,&rdquo; which I now understand was a *prediction*, not a *promise*.

## The recall

I clicked &ldquo;Recall this message.&rdquo; Outlook, ever the comedian, sent a *second*
email to all 417 recipients informing them that I had attempted to recall the first. Two
people replied to the recall notification. One of them was the CEO. He used an emoji.

I do not know what the emoji meant. I have not slept in days.

## The walk

I went for a walk. I came back. The emails were, regrettably, still there. They will, I am
told, *always* still be there. This is the nature of email. This is the nature of choices.
