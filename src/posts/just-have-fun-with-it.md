I was tired. I had not been sleeping well. I sat down in the chair and the barber said,
&ldquo;What are we doing today,&rdquo; and instead of saying any of the perfectly normal
things a human being might say, I said: &ldquo;Just have fun with it.&rdquo;

He nodded slowly. The nod of a man being handed an opportunity.

## What &ldquo;fun&rdquo; turned out to mean

- A length that is described, technically, as a length.
- An asymmetry I have been told is &ldquo;a *choice*.&rdquo;
- A small region above the right ear that my partner has referred to, gently, as &ldquo;the runway.&rdquo;

I tipped him. I tipped him *generously*, because I was in shock and because I have been
raised to be polite even when something terrible is happening to me in slow motion in a
mirror.

## In conclusion

If a professional with scissors asks you a direct question, give them a direct answer.
&ldquo;Just have fun with it&rdquo; is not an answer. It is a dare. They will accept
the dare. They have been waiting for someone to accept the dare.
